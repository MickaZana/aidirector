# AI Director — User Guide

## Overview

AI Director transforms long-form sports and podcast video into short-form clips optimised for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels. Upload a match or podcast, and AI automatically finds the best moments, creates vertical clips with captions, and prepares them for download.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Upload a Video

1. Navigate to **New Clip** in the sidebar.
2. Drag and drop your video file onto the upload area, or click **Browse Computer** to select a file.
3. Supported formats: **MP4, MOV, MKV**.
4. Maximum file size: **2.2 GB**.

> **Tip:** For best results, use clean footage with clear audio. The AI performs best with well-lit, steady video.

### 2. Configure Your Project

Once your video is uploaded:

1. **Select video type** — Choose "Match" or "Podcast" so the AI knows what to look for.
2. **Choose platforms** — Pick where you want to publish (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels).
3. **Set clip count** — Choose how many clips to generate (6–24).

### 3. Start Processing

Click **Create Clips** to begin. The processing screen shows:
- A progress indicator with estimated time remaining.
- A timeline of what's happening ("AI watching your video", "Finding key moments", etc.).
- Helpful tips while you wait.

Processing typically takes **3–8 minutes** depending on video length.

### 4. Review & Download

When processing completes, you'll be redirected to **My Clips**:

1. **Browse** your generated clips in the gallery.
2. **Preview** any clip by clicking it — a panel opens with details (duration, quality score, platform compatibility).
3. **Download** individual clips or use **Download All** to get everything at once.

---

## Features

### Quality Scores
Each clip has a quality score (1–10) based on:
- Visual clarity
- Audio quality
- Moment significance
- Platform optimisation

### Platform Optimisation
Clips are automatically formatted for each selected platform's recommended aspect ratio and duration.

### Continue Working
Recent uploads appear in the "Continue Working" card so you can quickly resume where you left off.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Upload fails | Check your internet connection. Files over 2.2 GB cannot be uploaded. Try a shorter video. |
| Processing takes too long | Longer videos naturally take more time. You can safely leave the page — processing continues in the background. |
| Clips look wrong | Try uploading cleaner footage with better lighting and audio. |
| Download doesn't start | Disable ad-blockers for this site. Try a different browser. |
